Academic journey

An admission I had to decline, and the independent learning I kept doing.

Admission and scholarship offer, 2026

Vietnam Maritime University

Undergraduate Marine Engineering, taught in English

I was offered admission to VMU for the October 2026 to October 2030 study period, with a scholarship covering tuition, dormitory fees if required, and student medical insurance. The admission notice states that airfare, textbooks, documents, and visa-approval costs are not covered.

Why I could not enroll

Although I was deeply grateful for this opportunity, my grandfather's illness created an urgent family responsibility. I needed to remain with him and help care for his health while I still had the opportunity to do so. After careful consideration, I made the very difficult decision to forgo this scholarship and my planned enrollment at VMU so that I could support my grandfather during this important time. This decision was made out of love and responsibility for my family, and it does not diminish my gratitude to VMU. I sincerely appreciate the university's support throughout the admission and visa application processes, and I remain committed to pursuing higher education when my circumstances allow.
